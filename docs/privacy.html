This Privacy Policy explains how Skinwatch ("Skinwatch", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Skinwatch Discord bot.

Skinwatch is designed to help users find Overwatch skins that may be available for purchase.

1. Information We Process

Skinwatch is designed to minimize the collection and storage of personal information.

When you interact with the Bot, Discord may provide information necessary for the Bot to receive and process your interaction. Depending on the type of interaction, this may include:

  • Your Discord user ID;
  • The Discord server where the command was used;
  • The channel where the command was used;
  • The command and its parameters;
  • Technical information provided by Discord as part of the interaction.

2. Information We Store

Skinwatch does not intentionally maintain a database of Discord users, server members, or user profiles.

Information received while processing a command is used to provide the requested functionality and is not intentionally stored as a permanent user record.

Depending on the hosting and infrastructure used to operate the Bot, technical information may be temporarily processed or recorded in operational logs.

3. How We Use Information

Information available to Skinwatch is used only when necessary to operate and maintain the Bot, including:

  • Processing Discord commands and interactions;
  • Returning requested search results;
  • Generating responses;
  • Maintaining and troubleshooting the Bot;
  • Preventing abuse and maintaining service security.

Skinwatch does not use Discord user information for advertising or targeted marketing.

4. Third-Party Services

Skinwatch relies on third-party services to provide its functionality.

Discord

Skinwatch operates through Discord and receives information through the Discord API as necessary to respond to user interactions.

Discord independently processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms of service.

OverHub

Skinwatch uses OverHub as a source of information regarding Overwatch skins.

Information obtained from OverHub may be used to provide search results to users.

Blizzard Entertainment

Skinwatch may provide links to Blizzard purchase pages.

Skinwatch does not process Blizzard account credentials, passwords, or payment information.

When you visit a third-party website through a link provided by Skinwatch, that website may collect information according to its own privacy policy.

5. Sharing of Information

Skinwatch does not sell personal information.

Skinwatch does not intentionally share Discord user information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the Bot, such as hosting or infrastructure providers, when required to provide and maintain the service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

6. Data Retention

Skinwatch is designed to minimize data retention.

We do not intentionally maintain permanent user profiles or databases containing Discord user information.

Temporary technical information may be retained in operational logs for as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, service operation, or legal obligations.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Because Skinwatch is designed not to maintain permanent user profiles, there may be no personal account record to delete.

If you believe Skinwatch has retained information associated with you and would like to request its deletion, contact the developer through the Skinwatch GitHub repository .

We will review deletion requests and delete applicable information where reasonably possible and legally permitted.

8. Children's Privacy

Skinwatch is not specifically directed at children.

Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and terms of service.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Skinwatch.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should never provide passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, or other sensitive credentials to Skinwatch.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to Skinwatch, applicable laws, or the way information is processed.

The "Last updated" date indicates when this Privacy Policy was most recently changed.

11. Contact

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the Skinwatch GitHub repository .

Skinwatch is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment, Discord, or OverHub.
